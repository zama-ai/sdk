## Cancelled

Absorbed into [SDK-134](SDK-134.md). Persistence via `GenericStorage` is now part of the foundation ticket — credentials are persisted from day one rather than shipping memory-only first and adding persistence later.

The decision to ship persistence immediately follows from resolving the at-rest protection question: the SDK delegates security to the storage backend (consumer's responsibility), with optional `derivationSecret` wrapping added later in [SDK-135](SDK-135.md). No spike or separate persistence ticket needed.

## Why (original)

In-memory credentials make every page reload re-prompt the user. For dApps this is the dominant interaction cost — a refresh mid-flow loses both the keypair and every permit, and the user pays the full setup cost again.
